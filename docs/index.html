How it works

Face landmarks. MediaPipe Face Landmarker finds 478 points on your face in every frame, on your device.

Eyes. The eye aspect ratio (EAR) falls when the eyes close. The limit is 65% of your own open-eye EAR and keeps adapting. Eyes closed for 1.5 s sound the alarm.

Mouth and head. A wide-open mouth for 1 s is a yawn. Head down with eyes closed is dozing; head down with eyes open is looking at a phone; head turned away for 2 s is a distraction.

Before the trip. A 60-second reaction test (based on the Psychomotor Vigilance Task) checks whether you are fit to drive.

Heart rate from the camera. Tiny colour changes of the forehead skin with each heartbeat give your pulse (rPPG, POS method). A clear drop adds to the fatigue score.

Your own normal. Each trip is saved to your driver profile on this device, so today is compared with your usual blinks, eye closure, pulse and reaction time.

Early warning. The trend of the score predicts when you will reach "very tired", so you can plan a stop before it happens.

Fatigue score. PERCLOS, slow blinks, yawns, nods, alarms, night hours and drive time add up to a 0–100 score with spoken warnings in English, Tamil or Hindi.
